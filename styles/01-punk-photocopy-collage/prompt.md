# 01 — Punk Photocopy Collage

**Look:** high-contrast B&W xerox cutout of the subject, rough white paper rim, offset orange/yellow/pink marker outline, black/white paper ground, big hand-cut red star, red scrawls. Boils every frame.

**Source:** the original reference prompt (Google Doc): https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1HA7DBqyw48jVrqBae_LVEvU5us7wJIlrJbLQS5lfl9k/mobilebasic

**Example:** `example.png` (6 frames of the test clip), `before_after.png`. Full render lives in the invideo project https://invideo.io/@alief-178654/punk-collage-edit
