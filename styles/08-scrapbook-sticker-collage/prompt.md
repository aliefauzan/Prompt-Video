# 08 — Scrapbook Sticker Collage

**Look:** each frame is a soft, cute journal page. The people are a pastel-graded die-cut photo sticker with a thick white rim and a soft drop shadow, sometimes a taped-in photo print. They sit on gingham, grid or dotted paper, or on torn kraft and pastel paper scraps. The page also holds washi tape strips, heart, star and daisy stickers, gel-pen doodles, and a handwritten caption on a torn label ("best day ever", "core memory"). Stickers, tape and doodles wiggle slightly every frame, like stop-motion. It is the gentle, pastel opposite of punk collage.

**Also called / where it trends:** "scrapbook edit", "digital scrapbook", "scrapbook trend", "junk journal", "sticker edit", "journal edit", "photo dump scrapbook". CapCut scrapbook templates and tutorials on TikTok ("scrapbook digital #CapCut" 78.9K), TikTok and IG journaling creators, Pinterest washi-tape collage boards, and the 2026 "handmade / dopamine-bright" design trend.

## Prompt

```text
FULL PROMPT FOR SCRAPBOOK STICKER COLLAGE EDIT:

I'm uploading one video clip. Convert it into a soft, cute handmade SCRAPBOOK / JOURNAL PAGE frame-by-frame animation: die-cut photo stickers, washi tape, pastel papers, cute stickers, gel-pen doodles and handwritten captions.
Keep the SAME frame extraction, timing, motion, composition, character identity and continuity workflow.
The STYLE ONLY should change.
Work through the stages below in order. Do not skip ahead.

TOOL LOCK
Use Seedream 5.0 Pro for ALL image generation in this workflow.
Do not substitute another image model, not even as a fallback.

CORE VISUAL STYLE
Transform the footage into a cosy scrapbook page built from:
* the people as a full-colour, softly pastel-graded PHOTO STICKER
* a thick, rounded WHITE DIE-CUT BORDER around the people
* a soft, short drop shadow under the sticker
* patterned journal paper: gingham, grid, dot, lined
* torn kraft paper and torn pastel paper scraps
* translucent patterned WASHI TAPE strips
* cute stickers: hearts, stars, daisies, sparkles, clouds
* GEL-PEN DOODLES: hearts, stars, swirls, arrows, little dashes
* a handwritten CAPTION on a torn paper label
* occasional taped-in photo prints with a white border
* journaling / junk-journal / digital-scrapbook aesthetics
The result should feel like someone:
PRINTED THE VIDEO FRAME
→ CUT THE PEOPLE OUT AS A STICKER WITH A WHITE BORDER
→ STUCK IT ONTO A PRETTY JOURNAL PAGE
→ TAPED IT DOWN WITH WASHI TAPE
→ ADDED CUTE STICKERS
→ DOODLED AROUND IT WITH GEL PENS
→ WROTE A LITTLE CAPTION
→ PHOTOGRAPHED THE PAGE
→ ANIMATED IT FRAME-BY-FRAME.

MOOD
Soft, sweet, wholesome, nostalgic, "best friends" energy.
This is the OPPOSITE of a punk / xerox collage:
* NO harsh black-and-white
* NO aggressive red graphics
* NO grime, toner noise or ransom-note lettering
Everything is light, airy, rounded and pastel.

CHARACTER — PHOTO STICKER
The people remain PHOTOGRAPHIC and in COLOUR.
Preserve each person's exact:
* identity
* face
* expression
* hairstyle
* clothing and its patterns
* accessories (e.g. glasses)
* pose
* body proportions
* movement
* hand position
* interaction with objects
Do NOT redraw the people.
Do NOT turn them into a cartoon or chibi illustration.
Give the photo a soft pastel grade:
* lifted blacks
* slightly reduced saturation
* warm, creamy highlights
* a gentle soft glow
Faces stay clear, bright and recognisable.

DIE-CUT STICKER BORDER
* a thick, even, rounded WHITE border hugging the outside of the group (vinyl sticker style)
* smooth rounded corners, no jagged cuts
* a faint glossy sheen on the sticker surface
* a soft, short drop shadow so the sticker lifts off the paper
* small gaps between people may be filled white, like one sticker cut around the group
This is a clean, cute die-cut. It is NOT a rough hand-torn edge.

PAPER BACKGROUNDS
Switch between these page backgrounds in sections of a few seconds:
1. GINGHAM — pastel pink or blue gingham paper.
2. GRID — cream dotted or grid journal paper.
3. KRAFT + SCRAPS — brown kraft paper with torn pastel paper pieces layered on it.
4. DOTS — lilac or mint paper with tiny polka dots.
Optionally, in some sections, show the people instead as a slightly tilted PHOTO PRINT with a white border, taped at two corners, still on the journal page.
The office background is replaced by the page.
Keep the people at the same position and size as in the source.

WASHI TAPE
* 2–4 strips per page, at casual angles
* translucent, so paper shows through slightly
* soft patterns: stripes, dots, checks, tiny hearts
* zigzag or torn ends
* tape may overlap the sticker edge but NEVER a face

STICKERS
* 3–5 cute stickers per page: puffy hearts, stars, daisies, sparkles, clouds, a smiley
* each with its own thin white die-cut rim and a tiny shadow
* placed in empty space around the people
* NEVER on a face

DOODLES
* gel-pen / fine-marker doodles in plum, pink or soft blue
* hearts, little stars, swirls, arrows pointing at people, dashes, sparkles
* slightly wobbly, hand-drawn lines
* drawn on the paper around the sticker

HANDWRITTEN CAPTION
* one short caption per section, e.g. "best day ever", "office besties", "us :)", "little moments", "say cheese!", "core memory"
* cute rounded handwriting (journal style)
* written on a small torn white or pastel label, sometimes taped
* correctly spelled and readable
* kept OFF the faces

PALETTE
Pastels: baby pink, peach, butter yellow, mint, sky blue, lilac, cream, kraft brown, white.
Accent ink: plum / berry for doodles and handwriting.
Do NOT use harsh black, neon, or heavy saturated primaries.

OBJECT CONTINUITY
IMPORTANT:
If a person is interacting with an object, keep that object.
Examples:
* phone
* chair
* bag
* glasses
* clothing accessory
If it is part of the action, include it inside the sticker.
Do NOT accidentally remove it.

DO NOT
Do NOT make it dark, grungy or punk.
Do NOT use black-and-white or photocopy texture.
Do NOT use rough torn edges on the people sticker.
Do NOT cover faces with tape, stickers, doodles or captions.
Do NOT misspell captions or write gibberish.
Do NOT turn the people into drawings or cartoons.
Do NOT use neon glow, chrome or glitch effects.
Do NOT create new poses.
Do NOT change anatomy.
Do NOT change clothing.
Do NOT replace anyone's face.
It must feel CUTE, SOFT, HANDMADE and TACTILE.

STAGE 1 – FRAME EXTRACTION (STRICT)
1. Read the source clip's frame rate and duration.
Report both to me BEFORE doing anything else.
2. Extract frames as follows:
* If the source is 24 fps:
keep every SECOND frame, discard the one in between.
Nothing else.
* If the source is 30 fps:
conform to 24 fps first, then keep every second frame.
* Target in both cases:
exactly 12 unique frames per second of source.
3. Hard limits on the extraction:
* Total extracted frames must equal duration in seconds × 12, rounded down.
* State this number BEFORE extracting and confirm it AFTER extraction.
* Do NOT extract every frame "to be safe."
* Do NOT extract at a higher rate and downsample later.
* Do NOT extract from only part of the clip.
* Cover the video from the first frame through the last frame.
* No interpolation.
* No optical flow.
* No artificial in-between frames.
* No motion blur.
* No frame smoothing.
4. Name frames sequentially:
frame_01
frame_02
frame_03
frame_04
...
Continue in exact playback order.

STAGE 2 – TIMELINE VERIFICATION (STOP HERE)
Before generating or stylizing ANYTHING, build a verification timeline.
Place every extracted frame onto the timeline as its own individual clip.
Keep them in exact chronological playback order.
Each frame must last exactly:
1/12 second.
At this stage:
Use ONLY the original UNMODIFIED extracted frames.
* NO effects
* NO styling
* NO sticker cut-outs
* NO pastel grade
* NO paper backgrounds
* NO washi tape, stickers or doodles
* NO captions
* NO Seedream generation
This stage exists ONLY to verify timing.
REPORT BACK
Report:
(a) Total frame count on the timeline.
(b) Total timeline duration in seconds.
(c) Original source clip duration in seconds.
The timeline duration and original clip duration MUST match.
If they do not match, something went wrong during Stage 1.
SAY SO AND STOP.
Do not compensate using interpolation, duplicated frames, stretched frames, optical flow or timing manipulation.
After successfully building and reporting the verification timeline:
THEN STOP AND WAIT.
Do NOT begin Seedream generation.
Do NOT stylize any frames.
Do NOT begin the scrapbook transformation.
Wait until I explicitly confirm that the verification timeline is correct.
Only after my confirmation should you proceed to the image-generation / styling stage.

STAGE 3 – SCRAPBOOK STYLING (AFTER MY CONFIRMATION ONLY)
Style EVERY extracted frame individually with Seedream 5.0 Pro.
One generation per frame.
Use the original extracted frame as the image input for its own generation.
Apply every style rule above.
Keep the frame count, order and 1/12-second duration exactly as verified in Stage 2.
Replace each verification clip with its styled version in the same timeline slot.
Do NOT merge, skip, duplicate, reorder or interpolate frames.
Do NOT restyle a whole shot from one keyframe.
Plan the pages first and list them to me (frame range, paper background, sticker or photo-print mode, washi tapes, stickers, caption text and position) before generating.
Within a section keep the same paper, the same tapes, stickers, doodles and caption in the same places.
If Seedream cannot keep the decorations consistent, generate the people sticker per frame and lay the page, tape, stickers, doodles and caption as locked layers around it.

CHARACTER CONTINUITY LOCK
The SAME people must remain recognizable in every frame.
Never change:
* identity
* facial structure
* hairstyle
* clothing
* accessories
* body proportions
* anatomy
* expression
* pose
* action
* position
Generation is for VISUAL STYLE ONLY.

PERFECT MOTION TRACKING
The STYLE jitters.
The HUMAN MOTION does NOT.
The underlying movement must remain exactly faithful to the source footage.
If a head moves, the sticker follows.
If a hand moves, the sticker and its white border follow.
NO floating sticker.
NO previous-frame silhouette.
NO delayed border.

FRAME-BY-FRAME STOP-MOTION WIGGLE
THIS IS CRITICAL.
Every frame should feel like the page was re-photographed in stop-motion.
Allow between consecutive frames:
* stickers to wiggle by 1–3 px and a fraction of a degree
* washi tape to shift slightly
* doodle lines to re-draw with tiny wobble
* the white sticker border width to vary slightly
* paper grain and lighting to flicker gently
* the caption label to nudge slightly
Keep it GENTLE: cute wiggle, not chaotic boil.
The result must feel like a LIVING JOURNAL PAGE.

The image should deliberately contain:
* soft pastel colour
* thick white die-cut sticker rims
* soft drop shadows
* translucent washi tape
* cute stickers
* wobbly gel-pen doodles
* cute handwriting
* visible paper texture.
Avoid harsh, dark or gritty looks.
```
